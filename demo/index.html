        Call a candidate now

        Talk to the AI interviewer in the browser, or have Pingo phone the candidate. Either way they are scored and ranked when the call ends.

        Talking in the browser uses your microphone, so allow it when asked. The phone number is only needed for a phone call. If the line or the mic misbehaves, a sample call scores a recorded screen instead.

        How the screen runs

        1. A warm-up, then the candidate’s own story and two questions picked from their resume.
        2. Role questions in English or Hinglish, whichever the candidate prefers. Ten minutes at most.
        3. The call is scored on the rubric and lands in the ranking with quotes as evidence.
